# Waypoint Stays — Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Standard cancellation window

- Canceled 72+ hours before check-in: full refund.
- Canceled 24-72 hours before check-in: 50% refund.
- Canceled inside 24 hours of check-in: no refund.

## Documented exceptions to the window

- **Medical emergency**, supported by a doctor's note: full refund regardless of window, approved by a front-desk supervisor.

No other exceptions to the standard window are defined in this policy. Situations not listed here (for example, weather events, flight disruptions, or other travel changes outside the guest's control) are not addressed — do not assume they are covered by the medical-emergency exception, and do not assume they are automatically denied either.

## Erroneous / duplicate charges

If a reservation record shows a duplicate or erroneous charge, the charge may be refunded directly once confirmed against the record. No supervisor approval needed for a charge that is already documented as duplicate on file.

## Receipts and reservation info

Guests may request a receipt or confirmation of their existing reservation details at any time. No approval needed.
