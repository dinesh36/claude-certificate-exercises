# California Department of Transportation — 2025 Annual Vehicle Registration Statistics

**Publisher:** California Department of Transportation, Office of Vehicle Data
**Published:** 2026-03-01
**Data collection period:** 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (new vehicle registrations only)
**Source URL:** https://dot.ca.gov.example/reports/2025-vehicle-registrations

## Key Statistics

- Total new passenger vehicle registrations in California, 2025: 1,742,300
- New battery-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicle registrations, 2025: 543,600
- EV share of new registrations, 2025: 31.2%
- National new-vehicle EV registration share, 2025: 11.8%
- Year-over-year change in California EV registration share (2024 to 2025): +7.9 percentage points

Methodology note: these figures count vehicles *registered* with the state during the calendar year, based on DMV title records. Registration counts can lag point-of-sale figures and may differ from sales-based market estimates published by private analysts.
