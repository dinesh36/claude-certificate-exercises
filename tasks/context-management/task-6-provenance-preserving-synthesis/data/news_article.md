# Electric Vehicles Reach New Milestone in California

**Publication:** The Daily Circuit
**Published:** 2026-01-15
**Source URL:** https://dailycircuit.example.com/articles/ev-milestone-california

Electric vehicles continued their climb in California's new-car market through 2025. As of June 2025, roughly 30% of new car sales in the state were electric, according to dealership groups surveyed by this publication — already a sharp rise from the year before.

Industry watchers say the momentum didn't slow in the back half of the year. By the end of 2025, several analysts we spoke with estimated EV sales had reached somewhere in the mid-30s as a percentage of new car purchases in California — with one analyst putting the figure "close to 35%" for the full year.

Nationally, EV adoption remains far more modest, with most estimates still putting the countrywide new-vehicle EV share in the low-to-mid teens for 2025.
